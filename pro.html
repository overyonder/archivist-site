Plain answers

Before you choose.

Can I keep using Archivist Free indefinitely?

Yes. Free includes cross-provider Canon curation as well as collection management, verification and launching. Pro adds the Atlas’s original historical interpretation.

What happens when my content-update year ends?

Nothing is taken away. You keep Pro, every software update, and the latest Atlas content released during your entitlement. Renew only when subsequent historical research is worth it to you.

Is Archivist a subscription?

No. The planned standard price is a one-time US$30 purchase. It includes a perpetual Pro licence and 12 months of Historian content updates. Later Atlas updates will be an optional purchase with their price shown before you decide.

Does Archivist include commercial games?

You supply the game software you legally obtain. Archivist configures and manages it.

Checkout is currently closed. It will open with the usable build, founding price, delivery date, support process and refund terms.
